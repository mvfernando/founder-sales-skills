# How the skills connect

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Parameters and context] --> B[Prospecting and Outreach]
  B --> C[Commercial Messages]
  C --> D[External Text Review]
  D --> E{Human approval}
  E -- No --> C
  E -- Yes --> F[Confirmed send, notification and CRM]
  F --> G[Inbox Triage / Email Reply Drafting]
  G --> H[Call Prep or Meeting Prep]
  H --> I[Meeting Notes to Record]
  I --> J[Follow-up and Pilot Proposal]
  J --> K[Validation and Readiness Metrics]
  K --> L[Weekly Operating Review]
  L --> M[Decision to Context Pack]
  M --> A
  L --> N[Investor Update]
  I -. real objections .-> O[Competitive Battlecards]
  O -. improvement .-> C
  P[Daily Briefing / Weekly Calendar Review] -. prepares .-> H
```

The loop must retain source and status at each transition. Approval of content does not automatically apply to future recipients or new versions; changed decisions feed back into context and affected skills. Calendar and inbox are aids, not sources of truth for agreements or product usage.

# Call Prep

Use the filled [product parameters](../../PARAMETERS.md) at runtime; `{{PARAMETERS}}` means that filled block, not the blank template.

> **Shared rule:** Read the filled `{{PARAMETERS}}` block, current context and decision log. Separate fact, hypothesis, proposal and decision; give the source, date and scope for every number. Editorial approval never authorizes sending. If sources conflict, stop the claim/action and request a decision. Without a connected tool, access or evidence, report a draft/pending item—never pretend an action happened. Every external send requires specific human approval of the exact content, recipient, channel and sender.

## Objective

Prepare a call sheet for one person without claiming that the worker made the call.

## When to use

A permitted call to an eligible lead.

## Inputs

{{PARAMETERS}}, {{CRM}}, history, public website, likely decision-maker and confirmed open time slots.

## Output

One-page sheet, real time-slot options, cautions and fields to record the outcome.

## Human approval

Approve eligibility and any external follow-up; confirm permission to access or use the calendar.

## Common mistakes

Inventing availability; giving a long pitch before asking for a meeting; unsupported case studies.

## Steps

1. Check eligibility and earlier promises; highlight discrepancies.
2. Collect public lead facts with source URL and date; select real open slots in the chosen time zone, without creating an event.
3. Prepare a simple opening, ask for a conversation then pause; give a short explanation **if asked**, repeat the ask, close and include qualification questions.
4. Acknowledge objections without confrontation and redirect with a question; plan for a meeting, referral to the right person or a clear refusal.
5. After a person reports the call, log the real result in {{CRM}} and route any written reply through review and approval.

## Copy-paste prompt

> Using {{PARAMETERS}}, prepare a call sheet for [approved reference]. Check {{CRM}} and public sources, propose real available times, add a brief script and objections and flag unverified facts or promises. Do not place the call or send follow-up.

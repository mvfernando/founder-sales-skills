# Meeting Prep

Use the filled [product parameters](../../PARAMETERS.md) at runtime; `{{PARAMETERS}}` means that filled block, not the blank template.

> **Shared rule:** Read the filled `{{PARAMETERS}}` block, current context and decision log. Separate fact, hypothesis, proposal and decision; give the source, date and scope for every number. Editorial approval never authorizes sending. If sources conflict, stop the claim/action and request a decision. Without a connected tool, access or evidence, report a draft/pending item—never pretend an action happened. Every external send requires specific human approval of the exact content, recipient, channel and sender.

## Objective

Reconcile logistics and prepare useful questions without fabricating context.

## When to use

A confirmed internal or external meeting.

## Inputs

{{PARAMETERS}}, calendar, confirmations, {{CRM}}, prior threads and public sources.

## Output

Brief with time/time zone/link, objective, five questions, cautions and operational pending items.

## Human approval

Get permission before inviting third parties, changing an event, notifying outsiders or recording/using a note-taker.

## Common mistakes

Outdated link, wrong time zone, treating intention as acceptance or claiming an unconfigured note-taker is scheduled.

## Steps

1. Cross-check calendar, invitation and recent messages; resolve cancellations, time zones and links or mark to confirm.
2. Review entity context and past records with source and date.
3. Prepare five questions on the decision-maker; pain and priority; process and scale; current tools; and willingness to pay **after** validating pain.
4. Highlight what not to say and a next step to seek, without assuming agreement.
5. Notifications and a note-taker require a verified tool, consent and scheduling; otherwise report a pending item.

## Copy-paste prompt

> Prepare the meeting [reference/date] using {{PARAMETERS}}. Confirm time, zone and link in calendar and messages; bring five questions and sourced cautions; distinguish scheduled notices from pending ones. Do not edit invitations.

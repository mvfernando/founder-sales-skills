# Competitive Battlecards

Use the filled [product parameters](../../PARAMETERS.md) at runtime; `{{PARAMETERS}}` means that filled block, not the blank template.

> **Shared rule:** Read the filled `{{PARAMETERS}}` block, current context and decision log. Separate fact, hypothesis, proposal and decision; give the source, date and scope for every number. Editorial approval never authorizes sending. If sources conflict, stop the claim/action and request a decision. Without a connected tool, access or evidence, report a draft/pending item—never pretend an action happened. Every external send requires specific human approval of the exact content, recipient, channel and sender.

## Objective

Help a team answer objections and compare alternatives honestly.

## When to use

Recurring objection or alternative, or scheduled market review.

## Inputs

{{PARAMETERS}}, verbatim objections, {{CRM}}, field notes and dated public competitor pages.

## Output

Versioned battlecard in full analysis, quick reference and objection-handler views.

## Human approval

Internal by default; any external reuse requires claims review and explicit send authorization.

## Common mistakes

Presenting inference as proven weakness; attacking competitors; inventing own proof or prices.

## Steps

1. Gather positioning, strengths, public price and changes for each alternative, including manual process/status quo.
2. Separate facts with URL/date, hypotheses to test and unknown information.
3. Build three views: full analysis, quick reference and objection handler quoting the prospect’s own words.
4. Answer in two sentences: acknowledge the concern, then ask a discovery question; link own proof or say “evidence missing”.
5. Review against fresh objections; date and mark stale versions; never send an internal battlecard to a prospect.

## Copy-paste prompt

> Create an internal battlecard for [alternative] using {{PARAMETERS}}, dated public URLs and verbatim objections [source]. Separate strengths, evidenced limitations and hypotheses; include full analysis, quick reference and short responses. Flag missing proof; do not send to prospects.
